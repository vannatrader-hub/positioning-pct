How the desk reads it

  • The line is the HY−IG excess spread, smoothed with a 5-session EMA, as a Z-score against its trailing 252 sessions. Widening pushes it up.
  • A red dot marks a session with Z at 2 or more while the spread is already narrowing. A green cross marks Z at −2 or less with the spread widening off the lows.
  • Elevated or Stress credit while Spot Vol Beta prints 2 or more is the possible SPX bottom the desk watches for. Credit Z alone is not a buy.

Invalidation FRED prints lag a session and holidays carry the prior close. The 252-session window can move a regime with no new event behind it.

Desk · FRED: ICE BofA US High Yield Index OAS (BAMLH0A0HYM2) and ICE BofA US Corporate Index OAS (BAMLC0A0CM), in basis points; S&P 500 close (SP500); Cboe VIX close (VIXCLS). Spreads carried onto S&P 500 sessions. After Collin-Dufresne, Goldstein & Martin (2001) and Gilchrist & Zakrajšek (2012).

Bullish above +2 Neutral Bearish below −2 Caution +9 Stretched −5

Mega-cap leadership tape. Above +2 the seven are pulling the index; below −2 they are dragging it. Breadth is how many of the seven are up over the window. Bearish with breadth under 40% is the risk-off read, strongest when Credit Spread Regime sits in Elevated and Spot Vol Beta prints 2 or more. The caution line near +9 and the stretched line near −5 have tended to mark index pivots in sideways-to-trending tape.
